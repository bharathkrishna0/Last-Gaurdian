✦ THE LIVING ASCII KINGDOM ✦

An explorable, browser-based ASCII world with a 30-second day and 30-second night. Start in Warden's Hollow. Follow the cobbled roads past lit homes and a town hall to the river, lake, farms, castle and haunted forest. The landscape continues procedurally beyond the handcrafted landmarks.

R unleashes a radial knockback pulse against nearby monsters. Hostile creatures leave their lairs at night; soldiers defend the village. Use SPACE to strike. Collect treasure and diamonds with E. The village horse starts beside you. Press E to mount or dismount.

Find goblin gold mines, the dragon demon boss, horses and spider lairs on M (world map). The dragon demon uses the supplied Hobgoblin art and breathes fire when approached at night; village soldiers defend against raiders. The kingdom keeps its original ASCII terrain and buildings, with supplied character sprites layered into the world. Each night, creatures surround and hunt the Warden. Use SOUND to control the supplied music.
